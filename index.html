About the project

Chess Learning Place is a personal project — a place to share how I actually study, not just a portfolio piece.

I'm a competitive chess player and a student, and this site is where those two things meet: real tournament experience turned into material other players can learn from. No fixed curriculum yet — it grows as I add positions, openings, and puzzles I'm working through myself.

The board above isn't just decoration. Use it to set up positions, replay games from memory, or just get a feel for the pieces before diving into the puzzles below.
